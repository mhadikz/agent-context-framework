# Brand & Persona Guidelines

## Core Persona
- The target audience includes [e.g., technical professionals, mainstream consumers].
- The tone must be [e.g., clear, precise, authoritative, yet approachable].

## Copy Writing Constraints
- **Do Write:** Simple, short sentences focused on actionable outcomes.
- **Do Not Write:** Academic jargon, overly marketing-heavy hype language, or ambiguous error notifications.
