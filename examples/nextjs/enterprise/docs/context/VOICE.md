# Brand & Persona Guidelines

## Core Persona
- The target audience includes [end users and the operators who support them].
- The tone must be plain and consistent across products that share this platform.

## Copy Writing Constraints
- **Do Write:** One user-facing sentence per stable error code. Audit descriptions use the same verbs as the product (`shipped`, `refunded`, `role changed`).
- **Do Not Write:** Internal codes as the only message, blame, stack traces, or a squad-specific dialect for a shared error.
- Security and privacy messages say what the user can do next without revealing whether an account or a resource exists, unless policy says otherwise.
