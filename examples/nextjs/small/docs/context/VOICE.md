# Brand & Persona Guidelines

## Core Persona
- The target audience includes [e.g., technical professionals, mainstream consumers].
- The tone must be clear, precise, and calm. Errors tell the user what happened and what to do next.

## Copy Writing Constraints
- **Do Write:** Short sentences. Stable error codes in the API, human sentences in the UI (`This order has already been paid.`).
- **Do Not Write:** `Something went wrong`, stack traces, SQL, internal module names, or marketing filler in errors and empty states.
- Validation copy names the field and the fix. Auth copy does not reveal whether an account exists unless the product decision says so.
